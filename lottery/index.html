01Sealed first

On load the machine draws a random 128-bit seed, shuffles all 55 balls with it, and publishes a SHA-256 commitment. Screenshot that hash and send it to the group before you draw.

02First ball takes the slot

A manager is seated the first time one of their balls comes out. Later balls for a seated manager are dead and just roll past. Ten seats, then it stops.

03Checked after

The seed unseals at the end. Rerun the shuffle from it and you get the identical sequence, and its hash matches what you published in step one.
